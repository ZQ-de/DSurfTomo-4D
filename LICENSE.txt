This software is based on the original DSurfTomo package developed by Hongjian Fang (2017). The present version, DSurfTomo-4D, includes substantial modifications for time-lapse surface-wave tomography, including common-path differential traveltime construction, differential inversion, and synthetic time-lapse velocity variation experiments. The Taipei ambient-noise dataset included in this repository was originally provided with the DSurfTomo package by Hongjian Fang and is used here for benchmark and methodological evaluation purposes. Additional modifications and new implementations introduced in DSurfTomo-4D are developed by Zhao Qian and collaborators.
